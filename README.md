## 🧠 Key Differentiators & Standards

- **Explainable Intelligence Hierarchy**: Strict visual distinction across:
  - `FACT`: 100% deterministic blockchain block or official complainant record.
  - `OBSERVATION`: Directly observed transfer velocity and topological structure.
  - `DERIVED`: Multi-case intersection through exact address matching.
  - `INFERENCE`: Algorithmic campaign clustering based on graph similarity.
  - `POSSIBLE`: Heuristic attribution to external VASPs requiring independent legal verification (LEIR).
- **Relational Mock Data Layer**: 100% internal data consistency across cases, wallets, transaction hashes, amounts, timestamps, campaigns, and VASPs.
- **Future-Ready Plug & Play Architecture**: Ready to swap mock endpoints for real blockchain APIs, graph engines, and Law Enforcement Information Request (LEIR) portals.
